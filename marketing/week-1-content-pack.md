## Day 2 — Outgrown spreadsheets  (Educate)

**LinkedIn**
Five signs your business has outgrown spreadsheets:

1. Two people edit the same file and overwrite each other.

3. Month-end takes days, not minutes.
4. New staff need a week just to learn "the file."
5. One wrong cell quietly breaks a number you report on.

Spreadsheets are where most businesses start, and they are brilliant — until the business gets real. Past a certain point, the workarounds cost more than the fix.

That fix is usually a small custom system: your data in one place, roles and permissions, and numbers you can trust.

We build those. If two of these five sound familiar, it might be time.

## Day 7 — Anatomy of a good SME website  (Proof / Educate)

**LinkedIn**
What actually makes a small-business website work? Not the design awards. Seven things:

1. Loads fast on a phone, on a normal connection.
2. Says what you do and who you help in the first line.
3. One clear next step — call, message, or buy.
4. A WhatsApp button that opens a chat, not a form no one fills.
5. Shows up on Google (Business Profile plus the basics).
6. Proof — real photos, real results, real reviews.
7. Easy for you to update without calling anyone.

Most sites miss half of these and wonder why the phone stays quiet. A good site is not a brochure. It is your best salesperson, working while you sleep.

This is the checklist we build against.
