# Harts Digital Hub — 100 Q&A for the Chatbot Knowledge Base

Written for a warm, human tone (not robotic/corporate). Company voice: "we/our", never "I/my"
(see [[company-voice]]). Intended as source content for a future `faq` table feeding
`app.js` / `api/chat.js`, the same way `services` does today — see [[chatbot-knowledge-base]].

## 1. General / What We Do

1. **Q: What does Harts Digital Hub actually do?**
   A: We build AI systems that handle the busywork around sales and bookings — answering customer questions, qualifying leads, and getting appointments on the calendar — so you're not doing it all manually or losing customers to slow replies.

2. **Q: Are you an agency, a software company, or something else?**
   A: A bit of both, honestly. We build the software (chatbots, booking flows, dashboards) and we also handle the setup and ongoing care, so you don't need an in-house dev team to run it.

3. **Q: Who is this actually for?**
   A: Anyone tired of missing sales because nobody answered fast enough — small businesses that need a simple booking bot, and larger operations that want automation wired into their existing systems.

4. **Q: What makes you different from just buying a chatbot tool off the shelf?**
   A: Off-the-shelf tools give you a chat window. We give you a chat window connected to your actual business — your prices, your calendar, your CRM — so it can do real work instead of just answering FAQs.

5. **Q: Do you only work with businesses in one country?**
   A: We're based in Cameroon and started serving businesses here, but our systems work anywhere — WhatsApp, websites, and cloud infrastructure don't care about borders.

6. **Q: What industries do you work with?**
   A: Retail, services, hospitality, healthcare bookings, and a growing list of B2B clients who want internal tools built around how they actually operate. If sales or bookings are part of your business, we can probably help.

7. **Q: How long have you been doing this?**
   A: We've been building and refining these systems long enough to know what actually gets used by real customers versus what just looks good in a demo.

8. **Q: Can I see examples of what you've built?**
   A: Yes — ask us and we'll walk you through relevant examples for your type of business. We keep specific client details private, but we're happy to show you what's possible.

9. **Q: Is this just a fancy chatbot, or does it actually replace work I'm doing?**
   A: The goal is the second one. If all it did was chat, it wouldn't be worth your time — ours checks real data, books real appointments, and hands off to a human exactly when it should.

10. **Q: What's the first step if I want to work with you?**
    A: Tell us what's eating your time right now — missed messages, no-shows, manual booking — and we'll tell you honestly whether automation is the right fix and roughly what it'd take.

## 2. AI Chatbots & Automation

11. **Q: What can your AI chatbot actually do?**
    A: It answers customer questions in real time, checks your live prices or availability, qualifies leads, books appointments, and hands the conversation to a human the moment it needs a real person.

12. **Q: Is the chatbot just following a script, or is it actually "AI"?**
    A: Both, blended. It uses a real language model so conversations feel natural, but it's also wired into your actual data — so it's not guessing or making things up when it answers.

13. **Q: What happens if the bot doesn't know the answer?**
    A: It says so, instead of making something up, and hands the conversation to a human. We'd rather it admit it doesn't know than give your customer bad information.

14. **Q: Can the bot talk to customers in more than one language?**
    A: Yes — our bots handle English and French out of the box, and we can add more languages depending on your customer base.

15. **Q: Does the bot ever sound robotic or scripted?**
    A: We specifically tune it not to. Customers should feel like they're talking to someone who works there, not reading a script — that's a big part of what we spend time getting right.

16. **Q: Can it handle more than one conversation at once?**
    A: Yes — that's actually one of the biggest wins. A human can only reply to one customer at a time; the bot handles dozens simultaneously without anyone waiting.

17. **Q: What if a customer gets frustrated or asks something sensitive?**
    A: It's built to recognize when a conversation needs a human touch and hand off immediately, rather than trying to push through with a canned response.

18. **Q: Can the bot proactively reach out, or does it only respond?**
    A: Both are possible — reactive answering is the default, but we can set up proactive follow-ups too, like a nudge to a lead who went quiet.

19. **Q: Does the bot ever go "off-script" and say something we wouldn't want?**
    A: We build in guardrails specifically to prevent that — it stays grounded in your actual business information rather than free-styling.

20. **Q: Can we review or edit what the bot says before it goes live?**
    A: Absolutely — we walk through sample conversations with you before launch, and you can request tone or content changes at any point after.

21. **Q: How "smart" is it really — can it handle unusual questions?**
    A: It handles a lot more than a basic FAQ bot because it's reasoning over real data, not just matching keywords. It won't get everything, but it covers far more ground than people expect.

22. **Q: What's the difference between automation and just having a chatbot?**
    A: A chatbot talks. Automation acts — checking your calendar, updating a record, sending a confirmation. We build the second one; the chat is just the interface.

## 3. Sales Automation

23. **Q: How does the AI actually help with sales?**
    A: It answers product and pricing questions instantly, qualifies whether someone's a serious buyer, and moves them toward a purchase or a human handoff — all before you'd have even seen the message.

24. **Q: Will it replace my sales team?**
    A: No — it handles the repetitive first-contact work so your team spends their time on the conversations that actually need a person, not answering "what's the price" for the twentieth time today.

25. **Q: Can it actually close a sale, or just answer questions?**
    A: Depends on the business — for simpler products it can walk someone all the way to payment or checkout; for higher-touch sales it qualifies the lead and hands off to your team at the right moment.

26. **Q: How does it qualify a lead?**
    A: By asking the questions your sales team would ask anyway — budget, timeline, what they actually need — so by the time a human gets involved, the groundwork is done.

27. **Q: What happens to leads that come in outside business hours?**
    A: They get answered immediately instead of waiting until morning — which matters a lot, because a same-day reply converts far better than a next-day one.

28. **Q: Can it upsell or recommend add-ons?**
    A: Yes, if that fits your business — it can suggest relevant packages or add-ons the way a good salesperson would, based on what the customer's already asked about.

29. **Q: Does it track where leads come from?**
    A: Yes — we can tag conversations by source (website, WhatsApp, ads) so you know which channels are actually bringing in customers.

30. **Q: What if someone just wants to browse, not buy yet?**
    A: The bot's fine with that — it doesn't pressure, it informs. But it also keeps the door open for a follow-up later instead of letting the conversation just disappear.

31. **Q: Can it follow up with leads who went quiet?**
    A: Yes, we can set up gentle automated follow-ups so leads don't just fall through the cracks after the first message.

32. **Q: How fast does it respond compared to a human?**
    A: Instantly, 24/7 — no lag, no "let me check with someone," which is often the difference between winning a sale and losing it to a competitor who replied first.

## 4. Bookings & Appointments

33. **Q: How does the booking automation actually work?**
    A: A customer messages, the bot checks your real calendar or availability, offers open slots, and confirms the booking — no back-and-forth, no double-booking.

34. **Q: Does it connect to my existing calendar or booking system?**
    A: Yes, that's the point — it reads and writes to the system you already use, so you're not managing two calendars or re-entering anything by hand.

35. **Q: What happens if two customers try to book the same slot at once?**
    A: The system locks the slot the moment one booking is confirmed, so the second customer sees it's already taken — no accidental double-bookings.

36. **Q: Can it send reminders before an appointment?**
    A: Yes — automated reminders are one of the easiest wins, and they noticeably cut down on no-shows.

37. **Q: Can customers reschedule or cancel through the bot?**
    A: Yes, that's built in — they don't need to call or message you separately to change a booking.

38. **Q: Does it handle deposits or booking fees?**
    A: It can, if your business takes them — the bot can collect a deposit at the time of booking to reduce no-shows.

39. **Q: What if my business has multiple staff or locations with different availability?**
    A: The bot can be set up to check availability per staff member or location, not just one shared calendar.

40. **Q: Can it handle recurring appointments?**
    A: Yes — for businesses with repeat bookings (like regular services or check-ins), it can offer or manage recurring slots too.

41. **Q: What happens to booking data — do I still get to see it?**
    A: Yes, everything flows into your dashboard or calendar as normal — the bot is doing the coordination, but you keep full visibility.

42. **Q: Can it handle group bookings, not just one person at a time?**
    A: Yes, depending on your business — we set the booking logic to match how you actually take reservations, solo or group.

## 5. WhatsApp-Specific

43. **Q: Why WhatsApp specifically?**
    A: Because that's where a lot of your customers already are, especially in markets like ours — it removes the friction of asking someone to download a new app or visit a website first.

44. **Q: Do I need WhatsApp Business, or does my personal number work?**
    A: We set you up on WhatsApp Business, which is built for exactly this — separate from your personal number, with the tools automation actually needs.

45. **Q: Will customers know they're talking to a bot, or does it feel like a person replied?**
    A: We're upfront that it's automated when it matters, but the conversation itself is designed to feel natural and helpful rather than obviously scripted.

46. **Q: Can I still message customers manually on the same WhatsApp number?**
    A: Yes — the bot and your team share the same inbox, so you can jump into any conversation at any time.

47. **Q: Does the bot work with WhatsApp voice notes or images?**
    A: Text is the primary channel today; voice and image handling depend on the setup — ask us about your specific use case.

48. **Q: What if a customer sends something urgent — does the bot know to escalate?**
    A: Yes, it's built to recognize urgency and hand off to a human immediately rather than trying to handle everything itself.

49. **Q: Can the bot send catalog items or images through WhatsApp?**
    A: Yes — it can share product photos, pricing, or catalog links right in the conversation.

50. **Q: Is there a limit to how many WhatsApp conversations it can handle?**
    A: Practically, no — it scales to as many simultaneous conversations as your business gets, which is exactly the problem it's solving.
