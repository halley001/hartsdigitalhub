No. This course is written for everyone who can read and write. No coding, no maths, no prior experience needed.

Roughly 6–7 hours total across 10 modules. Go at your own pace — progress is saved automatically.

AI Governance is about who controls AI, what rules exist, and what rights you have. We believe everyone — not just tech experts — should understand these rules. It's one of the most important modules.

It is a H@rts Digital Hub certificate of completion. You can share it on LinkedIn or your CV to show employers and clients that you understand AI. Each certificate has a unique ID you can verify here.

Yes — fully mobile-friendly. No app to install.

Yes — see Corporate Upskilling above. We scope a tailored cohort to your team and reply with a proposal on WhatsApp; no fixed pricing is published since every cohort is different.

How do I verify someone's certificate?

Every certificate carries an ID like HDH-AI-XXXXXX. Head to the verification page and message us the ID on WhatsApp — we'll confirm it directly.
