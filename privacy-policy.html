Privacy Policy

This page explains what information H@rts collects when you use our website, chatbot, or Training Hub, and how we handle it. Last updated August 2026.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us — we don't track visitors silently or sell data.

  • Project quote / lead form: name, phone number, and optionally email, service, budget and project description (sent to our team via the form on our homepage and Training Hub page)
  • Chatbot conversations: messages you send our assistant, so we can generate a reply
  • Training Hub account: your email (for magic-link sign-in) and, on first login, your name, phone number and country
  • Certificate verification: whatever certificate ID or name you enter to look up a certificate

How we use it

We use the information above only to run the service you're using: to follow up on a project quote, to reply to you in the chatbot, to sign you into the Training Hub and track your course progress, and to issue and verify certificates. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

Cookies & local storage

If you sign in to the Training Hub, we set two strictly-necessary session cookies (sb_at and sb_rt) to keep you logged in. They're marked Secure and HttpOnly and aren't used for tracking or advertising. We don't currently run any analytics or advertising cookies.

Your browser also stores a few things locally on your own device (not on our servers) to make the site work smoothly: your chosen language, a cache of chatbot answers, and a local backup of anything you submit through the lead or review forms in case the request to our server fails.

Third parties we work with

We use a small number of service providers to run the site, and your information may pass through them as part of that:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles Training Hub sign-in
  • xAI — processes chatbot messages to generate replies

These providers only receive what's needed to perform their function and don't use your data for their own purposes.

Data retention & your rights

We keep lead, chatbot, and Training Hub account information for as long as needed to provide the service or as required for our business records. You can ask us at any time to see what information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted.
