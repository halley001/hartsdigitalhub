Chatbots · The handover

The part most chatbots get wrong.

Three quarters of people say a bot cannot handle their real question. They are usually right, and the reason is almost never the bot. It is what happens next. The conversation gets thrown to a human with none of the context, and the customer has to start again from the beginning.

We build the handover first and the answers second. Your team opens a conversation already knowing who it is, what they asked, what the bot checked, and what it could not settle. The customer repeats nothing.

A bot that knows when to stop is worth more than a bot that never stops.
Live conversation

09:41Do you still have the black one in 42?

09:41Yes, two left in Akwa. Want me to hold one for you?

09:42Can I pay in instalments?

Bot stops. Passes to a person, with the whole conversation attached.

09:42Hi, this is Marie. I can see you want the black 42 held. Yes, we do instalments.

Chatbots · Try it

Answer this one by hand.

It is 3:14 in the morning and a customer asks if you have it in stock. Reply the way you would by hand: press and hold the button for about six seconds.

03:14Bonjour, vous avez encore le modèle noir en 42 ?

03:19Allo ?

04:02Ok merci quand même.

0.0s

You held for 0.0 seconds. In real life that 3am message waits until someone reads it in the morning, about nine hours, and the customer has already gone. A chatbot replies in about one second.

07 · Questions

What people ask before they buy.

Do I need a web app, a chatbot, or both?

If your team is losing track of orders, stock or payments, start with the web app. If customers wait too long for answers, start with the chatbot. If both are true, we build the app first and the bot on top of it, so it answers from real records. We scope before we sell and will tell you which one you actually need.

Can you work with the system we already have?

Usually, yes. We can build on top of your existing CRM, ERP, POS or the exports from your accounting software, or replace a spreadsheet you have outgrown. We start with what you actually run.

What happens when the chatbot gets stuck?

A person takes over, and the customer repeats nothing. We build that path first, before we build a single answer, because it is where most chatbots fail.

How long does a build take?

A full web app usually takes two to four weeks, depending on how complex it is and how quickly we get your content and data. A simple site is closer to one to two weeks. You approve each milestone as it lands, so you see progress all the way through.

Who owns what you build?

You do. On a one-time build you own everything outright: the app, the files and the accounts. If we look after it on a care plan, it is still yours; we simply keep it running. Your data is yours too, and we never sell or share it.

What does it cost, honestly?

Builds start at $300 and are quoted per project against milestones you approve. Care is $125 a month and optional. You will have the number before any work starts.
